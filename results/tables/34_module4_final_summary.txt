INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Soil biodegradation differed among treatments (one-way ANOVA, p < 0.001).
Trichoderma and the Rhizobium + Trichoderma treatment showed significantly higher soil biodegradation than the uninoculated plant control in the pre-specified Holm-adjusted contrasts.
Rhizobium alone did not differ significantly from the uninoculated plant control.
The consortium was not significantly different from Trichoderma in Tukey HSD.
Treatment B was excluded because atrazine was not added.
Results are restricted to the greenhouse experiment at 40 days and 1000 ppm nominal initial atrazine concentration.
