INTERPRETATION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Results are descriptive; microorganisms are not treated as biological replicates.
2. Inhibition percentages at 5,000, 7,500, and 10,000 ppm are repeated characteristics of each microorganism.
3. Growth percentage is not analyzed separately because growth and inhibition percentages are algebraically complementary.
4. MIC values are neither calculated nor imputed.
5. The empirical position of R and T is descriptive and refers only to this 31-microorganism collection.
6. No causal relationship between tolerance and atrazine biodegradation is assumed.
