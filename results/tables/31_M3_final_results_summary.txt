INTERPRETATION LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Predictions are restricted to experimentally observed treatment × time combinations.
2. The model does not support prediction at unobserved sampling times.
3. The nominal 500 ppm initial concentration is not treated as an observed t=0 HPLC measurement.
4. LOOCV evaluates prediction of an independent experimental unit while retaining other replicates from the same treatment × time combination.
5. Leave-one-time-point-out validation showed poor generalization of continuous linear and quadratic temporal models to unseen times.
